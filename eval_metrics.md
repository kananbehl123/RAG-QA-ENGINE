# Evaluation Metrics Framework

Metrics used for evaluating retrieval quality include:
- Recall@k (Hit Rate@k)
- Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
- nDCG@k (Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain)
- Context Precision

Answer faithfulness and groundedness are measured through LLM-as-a-judge scoring and citation mapping verification alongside Exact Match (EM) and F1 text scores.
